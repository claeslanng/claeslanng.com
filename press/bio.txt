CLAES LANNG - DJ & Producer, Copenhagen, Denmark

Claes Lanng is a Danish DJ and producer based in Copenhagen. He DJs full-time, and for more than a decade he has played clubs, venues and festivals across Europe.

His sets and productions mix genres freely: house, EDM, afro house and moombahton, blended through his own mashups, bootlegs and remixes. Whatever the night, the goal is a full dance floor.
