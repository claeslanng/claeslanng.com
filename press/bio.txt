CLAES LANNG - Open-format DJ & Producer, Copenhagen, Denmark

Claes Lanng is a Danish open-format DJ and producer based in Copenhagen. He DJs full-time, and for more than a decade he has played clubs, venues and festivals across Europe.

He plays everything: his sets move freely between genres and follow the crowd rather than one sound. His productions do the same, with mashups, bootlegs and remixes alongside his own tracks. Whatever the night, the goal is a full dance floor.
